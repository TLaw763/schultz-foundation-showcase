Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and riding with us.

How do I book?

Call us or use our website. Tell us where you're going and when. We'll confirm the details and get you scheduled.

What areas do you serve?

We cover the main routes and neighborhoods across the region. If you're unsure whether we reach your location, contact us directly and we'll let you know.

Can I cancel my ride?

Yes. Cancel up to two hours before your scheduled pickup and there's no charge. Cancellations closer to your time may have a small fee.

Do you offer group rates?

We do. Multiple passengers heading the same direction can save money. Reach out to discuss your group's needs and we'll work out the details.

What if I need special accommodations?

Tell us what you need. Wheelchair access, extra time for boarding, specific vehicle types. We'll arrange it so your ride works for you.

Are your drivers insured?

Completely. Full insurance coverage on every vehicle and every trip. Your safety and protection matter to us.

Ready to move forward?

Get in touch with us today and let's arrange your ride.
